        ABOUT TRĀṆAM

        A helpful signal, not a verdict

        Trāṇam is a student safety project designed to make common recruitment scam patterns easier to spot.

        What this demo checks

        The browser-based scanner looks for text patterns such as fee requests, urgent payment pressure, unrealistic promises, free-email recruiting contacts, suspicious forms, and requests for sensitive information. PDF, TXT, and MD files are processed locally; scanned PDFs require OCR before their text can be checked.

        It does not contact companies, validate recruiter identities, inspect websites or live links, or determine whether an offer is genuine. A low score does not guarantee safety, and a warning is not proof of fraud.

        Safer ways to verify

        • Find the company’s official website yourself and use its published contact information.
        • Check that the recruiter uses a verifiable company email address.
        • Read the role, pay, hours, and terms carefully; be wary of pressure to act immediately.
        • Never share passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, or card security codes.
        • Talk to a trusted mentor, college placement cell, or career adviser if you are unsure.

        Trāṇam provides indicative assessments based on the information available. It cannot guarantee that an opportunity is genuine or fraudulent.
